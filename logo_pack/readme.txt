HJ STRENGTH COACHING — LOGO PACK
================================

WHICH FILE DO I USE?

png-transparent/   No background. Use these for almost everything —
                   websites, social posts, anything sitting on a colour,
                   a photo, or a coloured garment.

                   01 primary-lockup-black    light backgrounds
                   02 primary-lockup-white    dark backgrounds
                   03 framed-black            stickers, stamps (light)
                   04 framed-white            stickers, stamps (dark)
                   05 short-mark-black        small uses, light
                   06 short-mark-white        small uses, dark
                   07 coral-chip-black        full colour only, light
                   08 coral-chip-white        full colour only, dark
                   09 instagram-ink           Instagram avatar (primary)
                   10 instagram-white         Instagram avatar (alt)

jpeg/              Same artwork with a baked-in background. Use only
                   where JPEG is required — some print portals, some
                   email systems, uploads that reject PNG.

All artwork is trimmed to its true bounds with even clear space, at 4x
resolution — suitable for print at normal logo sizes.


RULES WORTH REMEMBERING

- One coral full-stop per mark. The coral chip replaces the dot; they
  never appear together.
- Clear space on all sides = the height of the H. The padding baked into
  these files is smaller than that, so add space in your layout.
- Minimum sizes: primary lockup 120px wide, short mark 24px.
- Over photography, always place the lockup on a solid ink band, never
  directly on the image.
- Never stretch, outline, shadow, or re-space the letters.


STILL OPEN

The original wordmark's font is unconfirmed. This set is built in
League Spartan. If the original was something else, the letterforms
need re-cutting before anything goes to print.

Also not included: true vector files (SVG/EPS). A printer or sign maker
may ask for these. The PNGs here are high enough resolution for most
jobs, but large-format work (banners, vehicle graphics) needs vectors.
